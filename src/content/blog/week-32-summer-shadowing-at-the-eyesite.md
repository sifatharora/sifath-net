*Catch-up entry for 2026-08-26; written in October 2026. Reflections may refer to earlier experiences.*

This summer, I shadowed at The EyeSite in Seattle. Although my main career interest is dentistry, spending time in another healthcare setting gave me a different starting point for thinking about patient care.

## Exploring Beyond One Career

It can be easy to focus so much on one goal that every experience has to match it exactly. I want to leave room for curiosity, too. Exploring eye care gives me a reason to ask what different healthcare professions share and what makes each one distinct.

The questions I am taking from this experience are bigger than a single specialty: How does a provider explain something unfamiliar? What helps someone feel comfortable asking a question? How do science and communication work together?

## Bringing Those Questions Back to Dentistry

As I reflect on shadowing, I keep returning to the kind of dentist I hope to become. I want to understand the science, but I also want to explain it in language people can use.

My summer at The EyeSite belongs in this journal because exploring healthcare more broadly is part of exploring dentistry. I do not have to have every answer yet. For now, I can pay attention, stay curious, and let new settings give me better questions.
